# Business Case Template for Cultural EdTech Platforms

Use this structure when creating `BUSINESS_CASE.md` alongside your technical build. Investors and competition judges need both technical excellence AND business viability.

## Document Structure

### 1. Executive Summary (2-3 paragraphs)
- What the platform does (one sentence)
- Market opportunity (TAM, market size)
- Unique value proposition (cultural moat)
- Investment ask (if raising funds)

### 2. Market Opportunity
```markdown
**Market:** Indonesia EdTech valued at Rp 3.2 Triliun (2026), CAGR 25%
**TAM (Total Addressable Market):** 50M students (Indonesia), 300M (ASEAN)
**SAM (Serviceable Available Market):** 10M digitally-accessible students (Indonesia)
**SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market):** 100K students (Year 1 target)

**Growth Drivers:**
- Government digital literacy programs (Kominfo)
- Curriculum Merdeka adoption (coding as mandatory subject)
- Post-pandemic online learning acceptance
- Middle-class expansion seeking quality education
```

### 3. Competitive Moat (Critical Section)

**Why Cultural Integration Creates Defensibility:**

| Aspect | Generic Platform | Cultural-First Platform |
|--------|------------------|------------------------|
| Content | Universal coding concepts | 34 provinces × unique stories |
| Replication | Easy (translate + rebrand) | Impossible without deep cultural knowledge |
| User Connection | Functional | Emotional (pride, identity) |
| Virality | Low | High (province competition) |
| Government Fit | Generic proposal | Perfect alignment with cultural preservation goals |

**Example Moat:**
"Scratch and Code.org can translate their UI to Indonesian in 1 week. They cannot create 60 levels of pusaka legendaris (Keris Empu Gandring, Tombak Naga Sasra) with authentic cerita rakyat (Malin Kundang, Tangkuban Perahu) tied to 34 provinces in 1 year without hiring cultural experts. By the time they try, we own the 'Indonesian coding education' category."

### 4. Revenue Model

**B2C (Freemium):**
- Free: 30 levels, basic features
- Premium: Rp 50K/month, all content + certificates
- Target: 1% conversion (1K paying from 100K free users)
- Year 1 ARR: Rp 600M

**B2B (Schools):**
- Tier 1: Rp 500K/year (50 students)
- Tier 2: Rp 2M/year (200 students)
- Tier 3: Rp 5M/year (1,000 students, teacher dashboard)
- Target: 5,000 schools (Year 1)
- Year 1 ARR: Rp 10 Miliar

**B2G (Government/Provincial):**
- Provincial license: Rp 5-10 Miliar/year (covers all schools in province)
- National curriculum integration: Rp 50-100 Miliar (one-time + annual maintenance)
- Target: 2 provinces (Year 2)
- Year 2 ARR: Rp 20 Miliar

**Licensing:**
- ASEAN white-label: $100K-500K per country (Thailand, Malaysia, Vietnam)
- Corporate CSR programs: Rp 100M-1 Miliar (Telkom, PLN, Pertamina)
- Year 3 ARR: Rp 50 Miliar+

### 5. Financial Projections

```markdown
| Metric | Year 1 (2027) | Year 2 (2028) | Year 3 (2029) |
|--------|---------------|---------------|---------------|
| Free Users | 100K | 1M | 5M |
| Paying Users (B2C) | 1K | 10K | 50K |
| Schools (B2B) | 5K | 50K | 200K |
| Provinces (B2G) | 0 | 2 | 10 |
| **Total ARR** | **Rp 10-15B** | **Rp 150B** | **Rp 500B** |
| Gross Margin | 80% | 85% | 85% |
| Team Size | 5-10 | 30-50 | 100-150 |
```

**Key Assumptions:**
- 1% freemium conversion (industry standard 2-5%, we conservative)
- School ARPU Rp 2M (average of 3 tiers)
- Government deals take 12-18 months to close
- ASEAN expansion Year 3 (after Indonesia PMF proven)

### 6. Go-to-Market Strategy

**Phase 1: Competition Win & Social Proof (Q4 2026)**
- Win Lomba Kebudayaan Nusantara (Oct 2026)
- Press coverage (Kompas, Detik, Techinasia)
- Viral launch on social media (influencer partnerships)
- Target: 10K free users, 100 schools interested

**Phase 2: Pilot & Iteration (Q1 2027)**
- Partner with 100 schools across 10 provinces
- Gather testimonials and case studies
- Iterate based on teacher feedback
- Target: 50K free users, 500 schools signed

**Phase 3: National Scale (Q2-Q4 2027)**
- Kemendikbud partnership announcement
- BUMN CSR funding (Telkom, PLN, Pertamina)
- Regional sales team (5 cities: Jakarta, Surabaya, Bandung, Medan, Makassar)
- Target: 100K users, 5K schools, Rp 10-15B ARR

**Phase 4: ASEAN Expansion (2028+)**
- White-label for Thailand, Malaysia (similar cultural diversity)
- Localize: Thai royal stories, Malaysian state cultures
- Partner with local EdTech distributors
- Target: 1M users regionally, Rp 150B ARR

### 7. Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Strength | Weakness | Our Advantage |
|------------|----------|----------|---------------|
| **Scratch (MIT)** | Global brand, free, huge community | Generic, US-centric, no local relevance | 34 provinces of cultural moat |
| **Code.org** | Curriculum-aligned, Hour of Code popular | English-first, no cultural hooks | Indonesian-first, government-aligned |
| **Kodingmu.id** | Local player, some cultural themes | Limited depth (5-10 themes), basic UI | 40 cultural stories, 8K VFX |
| **Ruangguru** | Market leader, funding, distribution | Focus on test prep, not coding depth | Coding-native, deeper engagement |

**Positioning:** "Indonesia's Duolingo for coding — learn to code while preserving Nusantara culture."

### 8. Exit Strategy

**Primary Path: Acquisition (3-5 years)**

**Potential Acquirers:**
1. **Ruangguru** (valued $1B+, 2021) — Add coding to their portfolio, cultural fit
2. **Zenius** — Expand beyond academic subjects into skills
3. **Gojek/Tokopedia** — Education vertical play, ecosystem expansion
4. **Google for Education** — ASEAN market entry, cultural localization
5. **Byju's/Unacademy** (India) — ASEAN expansion via proven local brand

**Valuation Methodology:**
- EdTech multiples: 6-10x ARR (SaaS standard)
- Year 3 ARR: Rp 500 Miliar
- Exit valuation: **Rp 3-5 Triliun** ($200-300M USD)
- Cultural premium: +20% (defensible moat)

**Secondary Path: IPO (5-7 years)**
- Follow Ruangguru's path (SPAC or IDX listing)
- Requires: Rp 1T+ revenue, profitability, regional presence
- Timeline: Longer, higher risk, but potential for Rp 10T+ valuation

### 11. Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **Competition from Scratch/Code.org** | Cultural moat prevents replication; they'd need 12+ months + cultural team |
| **Government curriculum changes** | Maintain advisory board with ex-Kemendikbud officials, stay ahead of policy |
| **Low conversion rates** | B2B (schools) is primary revenue, not B2C; less reliance on freemium |
| **Regional expansion complexity** | White-label model lets local partners handle cultural localization |
| **Tech talent shortage** | Remote-first, tap into global Indonesian developer diaspora |

**Measurable Impact:**
- Students reached: 100K (Year 1) → 5M (Year 3)
- Cultural stories preserved: 40+ cerita rakyat digitized
- Teacher training: 10K teachers upskilled in coding pedagogy
- Digital divide: 30% of users from Tier 2/3 cities (not just Jakarta)
- Gender: Target 40% female learners (vs 20% industry average)

## Tips for Writing

1. **Lead with numbers** — Investors scan for TAM, ARR, margins
2. **Make the moat visceral** — "34 provinces of cultural stories vs Scratch's zero" is concrete
3. **Show you understand unit economics** — CAC, LTV, payback period
4. **Be realistic** — Conservative projections show you're serious (can always beat guidance)
5. **Tell a story** — "This is Indonesia's Duolingo for coding" is more memorable than feature lists

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Too optimistic** — "We'll get 10M users in 6 months" without distribution plan  
❌ **No moat articulated** — If you can't explain why competitors can't copy, investors pass  
❌ **Missing financial model** — "We'll figure out revenue later" is a red flag  
❌ **Ignoring competition** — "No competitors exist" means you don't understand the market  
❌ **No exit path** — Investors need to know how they get liquidity  

## Document Length

- **For competitions:** 5-10 pages (include this in submission docs)
- **For pitch deck:** 1-2 slides summarizing (full doc in appendix)
- **For investor meetings:** 8-12 pages (detailed but skimmable)

## Final Checklist

- [ ] Market size quantified (TAM, SAM, SOM)
- [ ] Competitive moat clearly articulated
- [ ] Revenue model with 3 streams minimum
- [ ] 3-year financial projections (conservative)
- [ ] Go-to-market with specific channels
- [ ] Competitive analysis (4-5 competitors named)
- [ ] Exit strategy with valuation range
- [ ] Investment ask with use of funds (if raising)
- [ ] Risk section (shows you're not naive)
- [ ] Social impact metrics (for ESG/impact funds)
